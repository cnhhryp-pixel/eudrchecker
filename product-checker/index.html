Important 2026 changes

Some familiar products changed status.

The 2026 delegated update did more than add products. It also removed or narrowed several entries, so older EUDR lists can now be wrong.

Soluble coffee

CN 2101 11 00 added; applies from 30 Dec 2027.

Frozen cattle tongues

ex 0206 21 00 added; applies from 30 Dec 2027.

Cattle hides & leather

4101, 4104 and 4107 entries deleted from Annex I.

Retreaded tyres

Retreaded tyres were removed; the update narrows the relevant 4012 coverage.
