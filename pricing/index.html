Pricing FAQ

Questions before paying

Is Professional €49 per month?

No. €49 is a one-time payment for one Professional report.

What counts as one report?

One company / product assessment using one set of core scope, origin, role and evidence inputs. Complex portfolios or multiple suppliers/products are better handled under Enterprise.

Can I use the free tools without paying?

Yes. The preliminary checker, CN/Product Checker, Country Risk Lookup, GeoJSON Validator, Supplier Checklist and basic report preview remain free.

Can I preview the Professional format?

Yes. The Professional Report sample is public so you can review the structure before purchasing.

View sample Professional report →

Is this legal advice?

No. EUDRChecker provides compliance-support tools and reports. It does not provide legal advice or certify EUDR compliance.
