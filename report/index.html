Professional Report FAQ

Can I see the report before paying?

Yes. Generate the free preview first and review the public Professional sample. You only pay if you want the expanded Professional report.

Is €49 a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment for one Professional report covering one company / product assessment based on the information supplied.

Does the Professional Report prove EUDR compliance?

No. It is a structured compliance-support report. It does not replace legal advice, official product classification, a Due Diligence Statement, or competent-authority review.

How is the report delivered?

The current static-first workflow uses manual payment confirmation. After confirmation, the Professional report is prepared in a PDF-ready format.

What if I need several products or suppliers reviewed?

Use the Enterprise option for multi-product, multi-supplier or custom workflow requirements.
