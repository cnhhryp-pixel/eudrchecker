Scope guide

EUDR commodities and product scope

EUDR centers on seven commodities and specified derived products. The legal scope is determined by Annex I and CN codes, not by commodity name alone.

01CattleSpecies limits, meat and current 2026 scope changes.02CocoaBeans, cocoa derivatives and farm traceability.03Coffee0901 products plus soluble coffee added for 2027.04Oil palmElaeis spp., palm oil and specified derivatives.05RubberHevea rubber and current downstream-product scope.06SoySoybeans and derivatives; sowing seed exclusion.07WoodTimber, wood products and material-specific exclusions.

Commodity match is only step one

After identifying the commodity family, confirm the exact current CN code and any “ex” condition. Then identify your role, production country, geolocation needs and evidence gaps.

Why Annex I changes matter

The product list can be amended. The 2026 update clarified commodity definitions, removed or narrowed some product entries and added new products with a later 30 December 2027 application date. Avoid relying on old static product lists.
