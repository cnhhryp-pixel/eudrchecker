Scope guide

EUDR commodities and product scope

EUDR centers on seven commodities and specified derived products. The legal scope is determined by Annex I and CN codes, not by commodity name alone.

01

Cattle

Live cattle and specified derived products.

02

Cocoa

Cocoa beans and specified cocoa products.

03

Coffee

Coffee and specified coffee products.

04

Oil palm

Palm oil and specified derivatives.

05

Rubber

Natural rubber and specified rubber products.

06

Soy

Soya and specified soy products.

07

Wood

Wood, paper and specified derived products.

How to use this page

First identify the relevant commodity family. Then confirm the exact product and current CN code against Annex I. Only after that should you decide whether the product is in scope.
