Country benchmarking

EUDR country risk lookup

Use the current EU benchmarking categories to understand whether a sourcing country is low, standard or high risk.

Low risk

Examples: China, India, Vietnam, Ghana, United States, most EU countries.

Standard risk

Examples: Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, Côte d'Ivoire.

High risk

Examples: Belarus, Myanmar, Russia, North Korea.

Always verify

Country classifications can change, so check the official Commission list before relying on a result.

Why the classification matters

Low-risk sourcing can support simplified due diligence when all legal conditions are met. Standard- and high-risk sourcing can require a fuller risk-assessment path and higher scrutiny.
