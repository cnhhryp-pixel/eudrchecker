Country benchmarking

Country risk changes the due-diligence path.

The Commission classifies countries as low, standard or high risk. Low-risk sourcing can allow simplified due diligence when all legal conditions are met; it does not mean “no obligations.”

FAQ

Common EUDR questions

Does choosing one of the seven commodities mean my product is definitely covered?

No. The current Annex I product/CN-code list determines legal product scope.

Does low-risk origin mean there is no due diligence?

No. Simplified due diligence can apply when legal conditions are met, but relevant information still has to be collected.

What about non-EU suppliers?

EU customers may still require geolocation, legality and traceability evidence from non-EU producers and suppliers.

Is this legal advice?

No. EUDRChecker is a preliminary compliance aid. Verify current EU legislation, guidance and your transaction facts.
