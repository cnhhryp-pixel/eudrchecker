EUDR geolocation notes

Geometry type matters.

For non-cattle commodities, production plots larger than four hectares generally require polygon geolocation rather than a single point. For cattle, geolocation relates to establishments where cattle were kept. The validator checks geometry quality, but it cannot determine whether your supplied geometry represents the legally correct plot or establishment.
