Which EUDR date applies to you?

Start with three questions: Is the product in Annex I? What is your role? Which company-size category applies? A downstream SME trader can have a different responsibility profile from an upstream operator even when the headline application date is the same.

What changed for products added in 2026?

The 2026 product-scope update added products including soluble coffee, certain palm-oil derivatives and frozen cattle tongues. Those newly added products have the later application date of 30 December 2027.

What should be ready before your date?

  • Exact Annex I and CN-code scope confirmation.
  • Clear operator / trader / supplier role mapping.
  • Supplier, product and country-of-production information.
  • Required production geolocation.
  • Legality and deforestation-free supporting evidence.
  • Risk assessment and mitigation where required.
  • The applicable DDS or simplified-declaration workflow.
  • A record-retention process for supporting evidence.
Do not wait for the deadline to start collecting plot data. Geolocation and supplier evidence are often the slowest parts of the workflow.

FAQ

Do all companies start on 30 December 2026?

No. Most micro and small operators have the later date of 30 June 2027, while micro/small operators already covered by EUTR use 30 December 2026.

Are newly added 2026 products already subject from December 2026?

No. Products newly added by the 2026 delegated scope update become subject from 30 December 2027.

Does a later deadline mean I should delay data collection?

Usually not. Supplier evidence, geolocation and product classification can require significant preparation time.
