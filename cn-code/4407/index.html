4407 and the EUDR

Sawn wood under heading 4407 is within the broad EUDR wood product family. Forest origin and legality evidence are central.

Why CN 4407 matters

Heading 4407 covers sawn or chipped wood products and is a common EUDR-relevant customs heading in timber supply chains.

What should operators collect?

  • Forest / production-area geolocation.
  • Species and material information where relevant.
  • Supplier and harvest-chain records.
  • Evidence of legal production / harvest.
  • Deforestation-free and forest-degradation evidence as applicable.
Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS code used for your product.
  2. Check whether the Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify country of production of the relevant commodity.
  4. Collect required geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow before the relevant market activity.
