1208 10 and the EUDR

Soybean flour and meal under CN 1208 10 remain listed in the EUDR soy product family.

Check another codeGenerate EUDR Report

CommoditySoy
Preliminary statusLikely in scope
Current scope noteSpecified soy derivative

Processed soy still requires origin evidence

Even though the product is processed, the evidence workflow needs to connect the flour or meal back to the relevant soybean production origin.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS classification.
  2. Check whether the current Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify the relevant commodity and production origin.
  4. Collect geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow.
