Does CN 0901 fall under EUDR?

Coffee under heading 0901 is a core EUDR product family. The practical compliance question is therefore usually not only “is coffee relevant?” but whether the exact product, origin and operator role trigger the applicable workflow.

What evidence is typically needed?

  • Country and plot of coffee production.
  • Geolocation linked to the relevant batch.
  • Producer / supplier identity.
  • Production date or time range where relevant.
  • Legality and deforestation-free supporting evidence.
Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS code used for your product.
  2. Check whether the Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify country of production of the relevant commodity.
  4. Collect required geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow before the relevant market activity.
