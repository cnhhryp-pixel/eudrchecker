CN / HS code guide

1201 90 00 and the EUDR

The 2026 update narrowed the soybean entry to CN 1201 90 00 — soybeans other than for sowing.

What changed for soybeans?

The soybean entry was narrowed so that soybeans for sowing are excluded. CN 1201 90 00 captures other soybeans, whether or not broken.

What evidence matters?

Plot-level production origin, supplier/farm identity, country benchmark, legality and deforestation-free evidence remain central to the due-diligence workflow.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS code used for your product.
  2. Check whether the Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify country of production of the relevant commodity.
  4. Collect required geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow before the relevant market activity.
