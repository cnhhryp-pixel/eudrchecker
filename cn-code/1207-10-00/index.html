ex 1207 10 00 and the EUDR

The 2026 amendment refines the palm nuts and kernels entry to ex 1207 10 00, so exact commodity origin matters.

Why is this an “ex” entry?

The code is not a blanket scope decision for every product within the customs heading. The relevant product must also satisfy the oil-palm commodity conditions.

Species issue

The current oil-palm commodity definition focuses on Elaeis spp.; other palm species can fall outside that commodity definition.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS classification.
  2. Check whether the current Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify the relevant commodity and production origin.
  4. Collect geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow.
