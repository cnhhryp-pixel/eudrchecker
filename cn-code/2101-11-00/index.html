2101 11 00 and the EUDR

Coffee extracts, essences and concentrates under CN 2101 11 00 were added to Annex I by the 2026 amendment and have the later application date.

What changed?

The 2026 Annex I update added CN 2101 11 00 for extracts, essences and concentrates of coffee. The newly added product applies from 30 December 2027.

Why this matters

Older EUDR product lists may not include soluble coffee. Importers and suppliers should therefore update product-scope checks and evidence workflows before the later application date.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS code used for your product.
  2. Check whether the Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify country of production of the relevant commodity.
  4. Collect required geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow before the relevant market activity.
