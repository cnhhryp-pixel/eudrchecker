ex 9403 60 and the EUDR

Wooden furniture under ex 9403 60 remains relevant for new products, while used and second-hand products are excluded.

What changed in 2026?

The furniture entries under 9403 were changed to “ex” entries and expressly exclude used and second-hand products.

What manufacturers should keep

Maintain material and supplier traceability for the wood incorporated into the finished furniture and confirm production origin for relevant wood inputs.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS classification.
  2. Check whether the current Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify the relevant commodity and production origin.
  4. Collect geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow.
