1802 and the EUDR

CN 1802 remains listed, but the 2026 amendment adds an exclusion for waste as defined under EU waste law.

What changed in 2026?

The 2026 amendment retained CN 1802 but added an explicit exclusion for material qualifying as waste under the referenced EU waste definition.

Why the distinction matters

Cocoa residues that remain commercial products or by-products may be treated differently from material that legally qualifies as waste. Confirm the product status before relying on the code alone.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next
