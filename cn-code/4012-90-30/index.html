ex 4012 90 30 and the EUDR

The 2026 amendment replaced the broad ex 4012 entry with the much narrower ex 4012 90 30 tyre-treads entry.

What changed in 2026?

The prior broad entry covering retreaded or used tyres and related products was replaced by ex 4012 90 30 for tyre treads.

Practical consequence

Do not treat all retreaded or used tyres under 4012 as EUDR-relevant based on older lists. Verify the current subheading and material conditions.

Important: This is a screening page, not a customs-classification ruling. Confirm the exact current Annex I wording and your product classification before relying on the result.

What to do next

  1. Confirm the exact CN / HS classification.
  2. Check whether the current Annex I entry is direct or marked “ex”.
  3. Identify the relevant commodity and production origin.
  4. Collect geolocation and supplier evidence.
  5. Apply the applicable due-diligence workflow.
