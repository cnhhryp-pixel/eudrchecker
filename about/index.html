About

Practical EUDR tools for real workflows.

EUDRChecker.com is an independent tool site focused on turning complex compliance questions into clear screening steps, source links and action checklists.

What we are building

The first release focuses on preliminary scope, role, country-risk and deadline screening. The roadmap adds product/CN-code checking, GeoJSON validation, supplier evidence tools and paid professional reports.

Source policy

Core regulatory facts are tied to official EU sources wherever possible. The site is not affiliated with the European Commission.
