EUDR and sourcing from Malaysia

Malaysia's current classification

Malaysia is currently standard risk in the EU benchmarking system. This means operators generally should not assume the simplified low-risk pathway.

Key supplier-data issues

  • Separate plantation or forest origin from processing-facility location.
  • Keep mill / processor data linked to upstream plots.
  • Confirm exact CN code for palm and rubber derivatives.
  • Validate GeoJSON before declaration preparation.

For processed products, traceability should preserve the connection between the finished or semi-finished product and the relevant commodity production areas.

Key point: Country risk affects the due-diligence pathway. It does not decide whether a product is in scope. Product scope still depends on the current Annex I CN code.

Recommended workflow

  1. Confirm the exact product and CN/HS code.
  2. Identify the relevant commodity and actual country of production.
  3. Collect production-location and supplier evidence.
  4. Validate geolocation and batch linkage.
  5. Apply the appropriate due-diligence pathway for the current country benchmark.
  6. Keep records aligned for the applicable declaration workflow.
