Rubber guide

EUDR for rubber

Natural rubber remains a core EUDR commodity, but the 2026 scope update changed several downstream rubber entries. Exact CN classification is essential.

Check CN / product scopeGenerate EUDR Report

Natural rubber remains central

Natural rubber under heading 4001 remains a core EUDR product family, subject to the exact Annex I wording and commodity definition.

2026 downstream-product changes

The 2026 delegated update removed or narrowed several downstream rubber entries, including retreaded tyres and specified vulcanised-rubber products. Older EUDR product lists can therefore be misleading.

Use the Product Checker: search your CN/HS code before assuming a rubber product is still in scope.

Evidence for rubber sourcing

  • Rubber-producing plots and producer information.
  • Country of production of the relevant natural rubber.
  • Batch traceability through processors and manufacturers.
  • Legality and deforestation-free supporting evidence.

Manufacturers using imported rubber

The finished good may be manufactured in one country while the natural rubber was produced elsewhere. The due-diligence workflow may need the production origin of the relevant commodity, not merely the manufacturing country.
