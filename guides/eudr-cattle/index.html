Commodity guide

EUDR for cattle

The 2026 Annex I update clarified that the cattle commodity is limited to specified Bos genera, removed several cattle hide/leather entries and added frozen cattle tongues from 30 December 2027.

Which cattle are covered?

The 2026 update clarifies that the cattle commodity applies only to cattle within specified Bos genera/sub-genera under the relevant live-cattle subheadings. Buffalo (Syncerus), bison (Bison) and other bovines are excluded from that cattle commodity definition.

What changed in product scope?

The update removed cattle entries for raw hides and skins under 4101, tanned/crust hides under 4104 and further-prepared cattle leather under 4107. It also added frozen cattle tongues under ex 0206 21 00, applying from 30 December 2027.

Cattle geolocation works differently

For cattle-related products, the relevant geolocation relates to establishments where the cattle were kept, rather than only a single agricultural production plot concept used for crops or forestry.

Evidence to organize

  • Species and product classification.
  • Relevant establishment geolocation.
  • Animal / batch traceability through the supply chain.
  • Country benchmark and legality evidence.
  • Deforestation-free supporting evidence.
Scope warning: older checklists that still list 4101, 4104 or 4107 cattle leather as EUDR products are outdated after the 2026 amendment.
