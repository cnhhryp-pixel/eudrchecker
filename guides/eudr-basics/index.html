Guide 01

EUDR basics: who, what and when

A practical introduction for importers, exporters, operators, traders and non-EU suppliers.

What is EUDR?

EUDR is designed to prevent specified products placed on or exported from the EU market from contributing to deforestation or forest degradation, while also meeting legality requirements.

Which commodities are involved?

Cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soy and wood. Actual product scope depends on Annex I and the relevant CN code.

Who needs to care?

Operators and certain traders face direct obligations. Non-EU suppliers may still need to provide geolocation, legality and traceability evidence to EU customers.

What does due diligence involve?

At a high level: collect information, assess risk, mitigate risk where required, and complete the applicable DDS workflow before the relevant market activity.

Current dates

Large and medium operators: 30 December 2026. Most micro and small operators: 30 June 2027. Certain categories use 30 December 2026, and some 2026 scope additions have a later date.
