Wood guide

EUDR for wood

Wood has one of the broadest EUDR product families. Exact customs classification, material composition and production origin all matter.

Check CN / product scopeGenerate EUDR Report

Wood product scope is broad

Wood coverage spans many Chapter 44 headings and specified downstream products. Some furniture, pulp, paper and other products can be relevant depending on the exact Annex I code.

Material composition matters

Do not assume every woody-looking product is covered in the same way. Material and species definitions can affect the result, including products involving bamboo, rattan or other excluded materials.

What importers should collect

  • Exact product and CN classification.
  • Species / material information where relevant.
  • Forest or production-plot geolocation.
  • Supplier and producer chain information.
  • Evidence of legal harvest / production.
  • Deforestation-free and forest-degradation evidence as applicable.

Wood-specific workflow risk

Complex transformed products may involve multiple wood inputs and suppliers. Product-level traceability should be designed before shipment rather than reconstructed only after an EU customer requests evidence.

For manufacturers: if a finished item contains wood from multiple upstream sources, keep the material, supplier and origin evidence separable enough to support the operator's due diligence.
