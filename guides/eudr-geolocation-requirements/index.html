EUDR geolocation requirements

Geolocation is a core EUDR information requirement. Production-location data needs to be complete, linked to the relevant product and usable in the EUDR workflow.

What geolocation does EUDR require?

For relevant non-cattle commodities, due-diligence information includes geolocation of the plots where the commodity was produced. If a product contains commodity from multiple plots, all relevant plots must be accounted for. For cattle-related products, geolocation refers to establishments where the cattle were kept.

Why geolocation is more than a coordinate field

A technically valid coordinate is not enough. The operator needs confidence that the location corresponds to the relevant commodity and product or batch.

GeoJSON and the Information System

The EUDR Information System supports bulk location data, including GeoJSON. A practical workflow validates GeoJSON before submission.

Use the free tool: paste your data into the EUDR GeoJSON Validator to check syntax, coordinate ranges and common geometry errors.

Geolocation QA checklist

  • Correct longitude/latitude order.
  • Valid coordinate ranges.
  • Closed polygon rings.
  • All production plots included.
  • Geometry linked to the correct supplier and batch.
  • Production date or time range linked where required.
  • Source data retained for later correction or review.

Common errors

Typical problems include reversing coordinates, sending a factory address instead of commodity production location, missing plots in mixed batches and reusing geometry without confirming the same plots supplied the current shipment.
